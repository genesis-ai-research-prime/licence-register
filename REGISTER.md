# The Licence Register

Genesis AI Research Prime issues exactly three master licences. Everything else is a sub-licence, granted down a chain, or a territory held at Prime.

## Master licences

- **A — Canada.** Genesis AI Research Canada Ltd., incorporated in Ontario 9 November 2026, banked in Canada; the group's commercial headquarters and licensing hub. The founder-to-Canada head licence is in preparation with counsel.
- **B — United Kingdom.** Genesis AI Research UK Ltd — incorporation parked pending the ARIA outcome (30 November 2026).
- **C — Japan.** Genesis Japan — a forming node.

## Sub-licences and chains

- **Canada → USA.** Genesis AI Research New York, incorporated by its CEO; the United States node, held as a sub-licence under Canada.
- **USA → South America.** TBD — held in the chain, ungranted.
- **Canada → ASEAN.** The Malaysia Hub: the Phase Zero package has been issued and the Hub instructed to proceed; the Discovery Fee ladder has been issued by the CEO of Canada. Signature and cleared funds are awaited; nothing is granted before them.
- **ASEAN → GCC.** Held back. The GCC is not granted, does not vest on signature, and enters the grant only when the Discovery Fee has been received in full as cleared funds and the benchmark is certified.
- **UK → Europe.** Led by the CEO Europe designate; a forming chain.
- **Europe → Africa.** TBD — held in the chain, ungranted.
- **Japan → APAC.** TBD — held in the chain, ungranted.

## ROW — Prime's retained programme

The Rest of the World is not a territory and not a fourth licence. Every territory not yet granted — including Mexico and the Caribbean, the non-GCC Middle East, India and Korea while forming, Oceania, and China, which is not on the launch map — is held at Prime, run by the Founder personally, and protected by Prime directly. Territories graduate out of ROW into a chain as they are granted.

## The protection cascade

Protection follows the same lines as the grants:

- The USA protects South America. Canada protects the USA.
- Canada protects ASEAN and the GCC — directly, not through the Hub.
- The UK protects Europe. Europe protects Africa.
- Japan protects APAC.
- Prime protects ROW.

Protection is a duty under the Lodestone Rider: it runs both ways, it may not be exploited, and a protector that looks away from a breach beneath it is itself in breach.
