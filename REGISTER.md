## The protection cascade

Protection follows the same lines as the grants:

- The USA protects South America. Canada protects the USA.
- Canada protects ASEAN and the GCC — directly, not through the Hub.
- The UK protects Europe. Europe protects Africa.
- Japan protects APAC.
- Prime protects ROW.

A protection duty over an ungranted territory is a duty in waiting: it sells nothing, promises nothing to any third party, and begins only when the territory is granted and its holder named by Prime.

Protection is a duty under the Lodestone Rider: it runs both ways, it may not be exploited, and a protector that looks away from a breach beneath it is itself in breach.
